Overview

My professional work spans semiconductor test systems, production data infrastructure, and aerospace hardware assembly. The common requirement is to connect a test or operational result to the behavior of the underlying system, then make the procedure repeatable for the people who use it.

Systems Engineer

San Jose, CA, March 2026 to present

I own qualification, calibration, and production acceptance across six board families, using Python, PyQt6, SCPI, GPIB, and NI-DAQmx to coordinate bench instruments and collect electrical, analog, thermal, telemetry, and firmware measurements. I have also transitioned test procedures to an OpenTAP front end and OpenHTF back end, so that individual procedures can share instrument interfaces and reporting behavior.

For measurements across four electrostatic correction boards, I developed a custom selector and its embedded C firmware. The selector uses SCPI-style commands over RS-485 to route measurement outputs and Ethernet control to the active board. When faster automated testing exposed power-up connectivity and IP-assignment failures, I used Vivado and register-level analysis to investigate the interaction between hardware initialization and firmware.

A separate part of this work concerns configuration control. I built a system of record for more than 1,000 items using Rust microservices and PostgreSQL, linking serialized boards and parts with installations, software, firmware, and configurations. During field and overseas deployments, I coordinate hardware changes and compare scans before and after firmware upgrades.
